Riverside or Hilltop

Both walks start at the station. The riverside path is flatter and shorter; the hilltop loop has the view.

Recommended Riverside, 45 minutes, mostly shaded.

Steep Hilltop loop, 70 minutes, 180 m of climb.

The hilltop path closes at dusk from November.
